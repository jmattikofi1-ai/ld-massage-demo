Specialist

Manual lymphatic drainage

A gentle, rhythmic technique that stimulates the lymphatic system, which plays a key role in your overall health.

Benefits of MLD
  • Detoxification: helps move waste and excess fluid out of the body, easing bloating and supporting the immune system.
  • Immune function: improved lymph flow helps immune cells travel more effectively.
  • Pain relief: may ease discomfort linked to conditions such as fibromyalgia, arthritis or chronic fatigue.
  • Relaxation: calming, repetitive movements soothe the nervous system and reduce stress.
After surgery or lymph node removal
  • Reducing swelling: encourages trapped fluid to move, helping with post-operative swelling and oedema.
  • Supporting healing: promotes blood flow and tissue regeneration around the area.
  • Scar management: may help soften scar tissue and improve its appearance.
  • Comfort and movement: can ease post-operative discomfort and stiffness, helping restore range of motion.

MLD is a complementary therapy and does not replace medical advice. If you've had surgery, please mention it when you get in touch. 90 minutes full body MLD is £80 (women only).

Who it isn't suitable for

For safety, this treatment is not suitable for people with:

  • Metal or electronic implants, including pacemakers
  • Pregnancy or suspected pregnancy
  • Epilepsy or seizure disorders
  • DVT, blood clots or thrombophlebitis
  • Serious heart disease or rhythm problems
  • Uncontrolled blood pressure
  • Active cancer or a tumour in the treatment area
  • Fever, acute infection or significant illness
  • Active bleeding or bleeding disorders
  • Broken, infected or severely irritated skin
  • Recent fracture, serious injury or major surgery without medical clearance
  • Severe osteoporosis or high fracture risk
  • Severe nerve damage or reduced sensation
  • Severe varicose veins or vascular disease
  • Recent invasive procedures or injections
  • Certain neurological conditions

Please tell Lucia about any medical conditions, medications, implants or recent procedures before booking. If unsure, check with your GP first. This is a complementary therapy and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment.
